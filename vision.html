Vision and Mission

"Learning is a treasure that will follow it's owner everywhere".
At Vivek Public School we constantly thrive to provide best education to our students along with high-end infrastructure that facilitates a conducive environment for learning. Our mission is to provide a healthy atmosphere to every student to learn in the best possible manner by way of our unique teaching pedagogy. Our vision is to produce individuals who are not only self-sufficient and independent but who are deeply committed towards working for the betterment of the society.
