Vision and Mission

Mission

1. Healthy and supportive learning atmosphere for every student.
2. To develop well rounded, confident and responsible individuals who aspire to achieve their full potential.
3. To support and nurture children's natural desire to be life long learners.

Vision

1. To produce individuals who are not only self-sufficient and independent but who are deeply committed towards working for the betterment of the society.
2. To promote 21st century skills.
