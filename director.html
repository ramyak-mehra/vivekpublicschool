Principal's Message

Principal's Message

Martin Luther King, Jr. rightly acknowledges the importance of education in his famous saying- 'The function of education is to teach one to think intensively and to think critically. Intelligence plus character is the goal of true education."
The idea of quality education at Vivek Public is not restricted to the four walls of the classroom. Our idea is to provide a platform to each child to realise his/her true potential and grow up to become responsible adults in the society. To help achieve this goal, our teachers put in their sweat and blood to adopt & adept themselves with best teaching pedagogy to bring out the best in every child. Our institution stands tall for its continuous commitment towards excellence, good academic record and holistic development of our students. We have achieved best results in both XII and X Board classes over the years which is a testament of the commitment of the institution towards quality education. Debates, declamations, poetry recitation, annual functions, sports-day, themed based morning assemblies, painting competitions form an impeccable part of a student's routine at VPS that makes our children confident and stand out in a crowd. There are no dearth of opportunities for sports enthusiasts as the school provides facilities for all sports activities like cricket, badminton, volleyball, gymnastics, table tennis, Gatka, etc. The name of Vivek Public School has grown by heaps and bounds over a short span of time due to the continuous support from the parents and consistent efforts of the teachers. Our vision is to take this school to even greater heights and continue our winning streak in future. May Almighty bless this institution with great success and progress in times to come.

With Blessings,
Nidhi Mehra
Director/Principal
