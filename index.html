About School

Vivek Public School run by Indus Education Society (Regd.) follows the noble aim of establishing educational institutions for imparting quality value based education to children. The school is located inside Heritage city at Airport Road, Amritsar. The school started in the year 2006 and is spread over a sprawling compus of 25 acres. In addition to excellence in academics, the school also aims to provide environment for co-curricular activities to encourage the overall personality of the students. The school is affiliated to Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). Delhi vide Code no. 1630484 up to Senior Secondary level i.e. (10 +2) for all streams Medical, Non Medical, Commerce and Arts.
We lay emphasis to acquaint our children with the roots of Indian culture and to raise them above artificial barriers like cast, creed & language
