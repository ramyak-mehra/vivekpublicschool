About School

Vivek Public School is located in Amritsar, the religious hub of Punjab, spreading over 2.5 acres of land, founded in the year 2006 under the aegis of Indus Education Society (Regd.). The school is affiliated to Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), Delhi vide code no.1630484 running classes from Nursery to XII (for all streams including Medical, Non-Medical, Commerce and Humanities). The school follows the noble aim of imparting quality education & providing conducive environment for holistic development of every child to help them prepare for a better life ahead. In view of our commitment towards excellence and quality, we ensure to provide our students with well-maintained science & maths laboratories, well-stocked library, smart-classrooms, Art lab, spacious dance room, sprawling playgrounds and best sports facilities like Cricket, Badminton, Table Tennis, Gatka, etc. We strongly believe that each child is unique and possess distinct qualities that can be best nurtured by providing ample opportunities for growth and individual attention. We lay emphasis on inculcating our children with the values of our rich culture and rise above barriers like caste, colour, creed, language, etc.
