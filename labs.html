Labs


Laboratories help students to get first-hand learning experience by performing experiments on their own. The school has spacious and separate labs for Biology, Physics, Chemistry and computer . All science labs are well- equipped with necessary apparatus and other important materials to help children perform all kinds of experiments. Practicals are performed regularly in these labs to help children retain their knowledge for a longer period.
