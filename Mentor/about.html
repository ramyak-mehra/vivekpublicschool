Principal’s Message

name

As we are on the threshold of the Golden Jubilee celebration of our institution in the field of education, I express my heartfelt gratitude to the great visionaries of CKDCS who established this noble institution as a modern temple of learning. I have been very fortunate to be a part of this premiere institution for the last twenty years as a Principal. The school which made its humble beginning in 1967 has undergone significant changes in the form of world class infrastructural development. The school is well equipped with state of the art facilities which provide an ideal environment for teaching and learning. Academic excellence along with co-curricular activities has been the hallmark of our institution. Excellent performance is not an exception but an expectation in this school. A strong requirement today is to simultaneously weave a firm moral fibre in students’ personality. The key to this goal is to pursue a value based education process. The practice of value oriented process acts as nucleus around which all our school activities revolve. Aristotle said “Education of the mind without education of the heart is no education at all”. Therefore we endow our students with sensitivity and empathy which enables them to reach out to the society in fruitful service and untold creativity. So as we impart education to match the advancement in technology and globalization, we march our children ahead with ethos of moral values and principles. I feel proud to express that the glorious achievements of Sri Guru Harkrishan Sr. Sec. School G.T. Road now hardly needs any elaboration because a tree is known by its fruit. The clear vision of our inspiring management, the commitment and dedication of the teaching faculty, diligence of the students and co-operation of the parents, together has branded this school as one of the most sought after premier educational institution in the city. Much has been done in the last five decades and still more needs to be done in the years to come. Our journey towards excellence continues…… Wishing you all the best! Be blessed. Dr. Dharam Veer Singh Principal/Director National Awardee
